# 🎨 HealthPredict Pro — Brand Guide  
**Directory:** `docs/branding/`

---

# 🎯 Purpose

This guide defines the **visual identity**, **voice**, and **presentation standards** for HealthPredict Pro.  
It ensures consistency across UI, pitch decks, documentation, and marketing assets.

---

# 🪪 Brand Identity

### **Brand Name:**  
**HealthPredict Pro**

### **Tagline:**  
**“AI-Powered Insights for Better Care.”**

### **Core Values:**  
- Trust  
- Health  
- Transparency  
- Responsibility  
- Innovation  

---

# 🎨 Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|------|--------|
| Primary Blue | `#2563EB` | CTAs, headers, charts |
| Health Green | `#16A34A` | Success indicators, low risk |
| Alert Orange | `#F97316` | Medium risk indicators |
| Alert Red | `#DC2626` | High risk indicators |
| Neutral Gray | `#6B7280` | Text, icons, labels |
| Background Light | `#F9FAFB` | App backgrounds |

---

# 🖋 Typography

| Element | Font |
|---------|-------|
| Headings | **Inter Bold** |
| Body | **Inter Regular** |
| UI Labels | **Inter Medium** |

Inter is chosen because:  
- Clean  
- Professional  
- Used widely in dashboards  

---

# 🧩 Logo Guidelines

### **Logo Concept:**  
A shield + heartbeat line + AI circuit outline  
(Healthcare + protection + intelligence)

### Variants:
- Full logo (icon + text)  
- Icon-only (for favicon & app bar)  
- White on dark background  
- Color on light background  

Place logo files in:  
`docs/branding/assets/logo/`

---

# 🗣 Brand Voice

### Tone:
- Clear  
- Empathetic  
- Professional  
- Responsible  

### Style:
- Use simple language  
- Avoid medical jargon  
- Maintain trust and clarity  
- Avoid exaggeration  

---

# 📱 UI/UX Direction

### Style Principles:
- Clean clinical interface  
- High contrast for accessibility  
- Minimal but informative layout  
- Clear CTAs  
- Health-tech modern look  

### Consistency Assets:
- UI button styles → store in  
  `docs/branding/ui_elements/buttons.md`
- Card templates →  
  `docs/branding/ui_elements/cards.md`

---

# 🎯 Outcome

The branding guide ensures:
- Investors see professionalism  
- Users understand the UI easily  
- The platform feels trustworthy and health-focused  
- All screens (web, pitch deck, docs) match visually
